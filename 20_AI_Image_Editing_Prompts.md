# 20 AI Image Editing Prompts

## 1. Enhance Image Quality

**Prompt:** Improve sharpness, lighting, colors, and details for maximum
clarity and realism.

## 2. Change Background

**Prompt:** Replace the background seamlessly while keeping the subject
natural.

## 3. Color Correction

**Prompt:** Optimize colors, contrast, brightness, and skin tones for a
vibrant, balanced look.

## 4. Remove Objects

**Prompt:** Remove unwanted objects or people and reconstruct the scene
naturally.

## 5. Add Text to Image

**Prompt:** Add bold, modern text with strong hierarchy that enhances
impact.

## 6. Change Colors

**Prompt:** Change colors of objects while keeping textures, lighting,
and realism intact.

## 7. Resize Image

**Prompt:** Resize to any dimension or aspect ratio while preserving
quality.

## 8. Add Realistic Shadows

**Prompt:** Add natural shadows that match the lighting, perspective,
and depth.

## 9. Transform Style

**Prompt:** Turn the image into a cinematic, anime, 3D, painting, or
other artistic style.

## 10. Make It Perfect

**Prompt:** Refine every detail for a flawless, professional,
high-quality final result.

## 11. Professional Headshot

**Prompt:** Turn any casual photo into a polished studio headshot with a
clean backdrop.

## 12. Change Outfit

**Prompt:** Replace clothing with a new outfit while keeping body
posture natural.

## 13. Whiten Product Shot

**Prompt:** Turn any product photo into a clean white-background listing
image.

## 14. Restore Old Photo

**Prompt:** Repair scratches, fading, and blur from an old photo while
preserving original details.

## 15. Relight Scene

**Prompt:** Change the lighting direction, warmth, or mood of a photo.

## 16. Blur Background

**Prompt:** Add a soft cinematic blur behind the subject while keeping
the subject sharp.

## 17. Upscale Resolution

**Prompt:** Increase resolution and sharpness of a low-quality image
while keeping it natural.

## 18. Fix Facial Expression

**Prompt:** Adjust a facial expression slightly, such as a smile or open
eyes, naturally.

## 19. Change Time of Day

**Prompt:** Shift the scene lighting to sunrise, sunset, or night
without changing the setup.

## 20. Add Weather Effect

**Prompt:** Add rain, snow, fog, or light rays while keeping the subject
and setting realistic.
