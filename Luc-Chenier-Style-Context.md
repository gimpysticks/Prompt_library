# Luc Chenier (@luc.chenier) — Artistic Style Context

> For use as prompt context / style reference. Artist based in Québec, Canada. Works in digital and AI-assisted illustration.

## Core Identity
- Artist: Luc Chenier (@luc.chenier)
- Medium: Digital illustration, AI-assisted drawing, photomontage
- Origin: Québec, Canada
- Signature feeling: Melancholic solitude, dreamlike unease, beautiful but unsettling

## Overall Visual Language
- Hyper-detailed, photorealistic rendering that reads as classical oil painting + high-fashion photography
- Obsessive texture work: hair, sheer fabric, feathers, bark, fog, weathered stone
- Frequent motif of scale dissonance: tiny solitary figure against vast, impossible environment or creature
- Cinematic, desaturated lighting — dawn fog, pale overcast, chiaroscuro dust

## Two Modes

### Mode 1: Ethereal Gothic / Surreal Minimalism
Use when you want the windswept white gown on a cliff look.

- **Subject:** Solitary woman in flowing white (sometimes black) gown, porcelain skin, elongated silhouette
- **Environment:** Floating islands, isolated ruined cottage on cliff, dead trees, endless shallow still water, heavy mist
- **Movement:** Hair and fabric constantly windblown, merging with fog
- **Palette:** Bone white, stone grey, cool mist blue, almost monochrome. Very low saturation.
- **Composition:** Wide negative space, centered small figure, low horizon, floating / levitating architecture
- **References:** Romanticism, Caspar David Friedrich, gothic minimalism

### Mode 2: Dark Fantasy / Grotesque Baroque
Use when you want the creature / demon / pirate specter look.

- **Subject:** Hybrid creatures — human + peacock, bird legs, multi-limbed, horned, stilt-legged demons; emaciated undead figures, corrupted saints
- **Attributes:** Stretched limbs, hollow faces, iridescent feathers/fur, ritual daggers/swords, folk horror props
- **Environment:** Apocalyptic, muddy, organic decay — fog, rotting ships, baroque ruins
- **Palette:** Umber, ochre, rust, charcoal, deep iridescent teal/blue/black. Like an old painting left in dust.
- **Composition:** Tighter, theatrical, figure-dominated, Bosch-like density
- **References:** Hieronymus Bosch, Zdzisław Beksiński, Guillermo del Toro creature design, blasphemous iconography (reimagined, not literal)

## Technical Style Markers for Prompting
- Style keywords: `surreal digital painting, ethereal gothic, photorealistic, ultra-detailed, melancholic, windswept, foggy, isolated, baroque decay`
- Lighting: `soft diffuse fog light, volumetric mist, pale desaturated`
- Texture: `intricate fabric folds, flowing hair, feather detail, weathered stone`
- Mood: `lonely, dreamlike, haunting, elegiac, uncanny`
- Aspect: Often vertical/portrait for Mode 1, cinematic for Mode 2

## Negative Prompts / What to Avoid
- Avoid bright saturated colors, cartoon style, clean studio lighting, happy/cute expressions, modern urban settings — breaks the style
- Avoid over-sexualized framing — keep nudity artistic and mythological if present, not pin-up

## Example Usage in Your Prompt

```

Ethereal Gothic Mode, solitary figure in white gown on floating ruined house, 
windswept hair merging with fog, ultra-detailed photomontage, desaturated bone-white palette
```

```

```
