# Eight ChatGPT Prompts

## Source

- **Account:** AI News & Tools

## Cover

> These 8 ChatGPT prompts work so well, they’re almost scary.

## 1. The 20% Learning Shortcut

> I want to learn [topic].  
> Teach me only the most important 20% that will help me understand 80% of everything else.

## 2. Instant Text Improver

> Here is my text:  
> [paste text]  
> Make it clearer, smoother, and more powerful.  
> Show me exactly what you changed and explain why.

## 3. Research Mode

> I’m writing a report about [topic].  
> Research the subject, then rewrite it for a complete beginner. Keep it simple, accurate, and easy to understand.

## 4. Learn a Skill in 30 Days

> I want to learn [skill] from scratch.  
> Create a 30-day step-by-step plan that takes me from beginner to confident.

## 5. Smart Quiz Creator

> I’m studying [topic].  
> Ask me questions to test my understanding.  
> Correct my answers, explain my mistakes, and give examples to help me improve faster.

## 6. Prompt Generator

> Create a list of prompts I can use to help me achieve [specific goal].  
> Make them short, powerful, and practical.

## 7. Mastery Roadmap Sheet

> I want to become good at [topic].  
> Give me a simple roadmap with clear steps, what to practice, and how much time each phase will take.

## 8. Problem Solving Expert

> You are an expert in [area]. Guide me to solve [problem] with a direct approach, step-by-step.
