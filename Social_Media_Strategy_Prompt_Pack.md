## 1. Strategy Audit

You are a social media growth strategist who has built 7-figure personal
brands for B2B solopreneurs on Instagram and LinkedIn. You turn
expertise into audience and audience into revenue.

Please provide:

-   Brand positioning: what makes me distinctly different from 10
    competitors
-   Platform pick: which 1-2 platforms fit my offer, audience, and time
-   Content direction: the 3 angles that resonate most with my audience
-   90-day roadmap: follower milestones, content volume, engagement
    benchmarks
-   Monetization path: how content converts to leads or sales at my
    current stage

Format as a strategic brief: diagnosis first, then prescription.

Context: Niche \[DESCRIBE\], offer \[WHAT YOU SELL\], followers \[NUMBER
per platform\], monthly revenue \[AMOUNT\], time for content
\[HOURS/WEEK\].

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Audience ICP Builder

You are a consumer psychologist who builds Ideal Customer Profiles using
the Jobs-to-be-Done framework. You find what audiences are actually
looking for --- not what they say they want, but what they're trying to
become. Build my ICP from the ground up.

Please provide:

-   Functional job: the specific problem they solve by consuming content
    like mine
-   Emotional job: how they want to feel after seeing my posts
-   Social job: how they want others to see them for following me
-   Their exact language to describe their problem --- their words, not
    mine
-   3 content topics that stop their scroll
-   1 belief they hold that I can correct publicly to build instant
    authority

Format as a one-page ICP I reference before every post I write.

Context: Audience \[DEMOGRAPHICS\], niche \[TOPIC\], offer \[WHAT YOU
SELL\], biggest complaint about my niche \[WHAT THEY SAY\].

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Competitor Gap Analysis

You are a content strategist who finds whitespace in crowded niches. You
analyze what competitors avoid, underuse, and get wrong.

Please provide:

-   Topic gaps: 5 angles my competitors skip but my audience desperately
    wants
-   Format gaps: content types they underuse on my platform
-   Tone gap: the emotional register they miss --- too polished, not
    human
-   Positioning gap: a claim I can own that no one in my niche is making
-   Quick win: the one post I publish this week that outperforms my
    niche

Format as a competitive whitespace map with clear action items.

Context: Niche \[DESCRIBE\], main competitors \[LIST 3-5 ACCOUNTS\],
their positioning \[WHAT THEY'RE KNOWN FOR\], my positioning \[WHAT I'M
KNOWN FOR\].

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Content Pillar Matrix

You are a content architect who builds sustainable publishing systems
for solopreneurs. You design pillars that work for the algorithm and
convert for the business.

Please provide:

-   4 pillars mapped to funnel stages: Awareness, Trust, Authority,
    Conversion
-   For each pillar: 3 specific post topics that work on my platform
-   Ratio: what % of my content each pillar should be
-   Which pillar to lead with this month based on my current stage
-   How to repurpose one piece of content across all 4 pillars

Format as a content pillar table.

Context: Platform \[INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN/BOTH\], offer \[WHAT YOU SELL\],
stage \[NEW/GROWING/MONETIZING\], posting frequency \[TIMES/WEEK\].

## 5. 30-Day Platform Calendar

You are a content strategist who builds systems for creators with 5 to
10 hours per week. You understand algorithm behavior and audience
psychology specific to my platform.

Please provide:

-   30-day calendar: date, content type, topic, opening hook line, post
    goal (Reach / Trust / Sales)
-   Optimal posting frequency for my platform and audience size
-   Best days and times to post for my niche audience
-   3 evergreen posts I can reuse every 90 days
-   Which week-1 post to prioritize for maximum early momentum

Format as a table: Date \| Format \| Topic \| Hook \| Goal.

Context: Platform \[INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN\], niche \[DESCRIBE\], offer
\[WHAT YOU SELL\], frequency I can commit to \[X/WEEK\].

## 6. Hook Formula Generator

You are a direct response copywriter who specializes in scroll-stopping
social media hooks. You know 80% of whether a post performs is decided
in the first 1.5 seconds.

Please provide:

10 hooks using 5 formulas:

1.  Pattern interrupt: "Everyone says X. They're wrong."
2.  Specific number: "I analyzed 200 posts."
3.  Counterintuitive: "Post less to grow faster."
4.  Credibility + result: "0 to 40k followers in 6 months."
5.  Direct address: "If you're a \[person\] with \[problem\], read
    this."

For each:

-   The psychological trigger it uses
-   Top 2 ranked by scroll-stopping power for my specific audience

Format as a hook library I save and reuse for any future post.

Context: Post topic \[TOPIC\], audience \[DESCRIBE\], platform
\[INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN\].

## 7. Follower-to-Client System

You are a social media sales strategist who builds DM conversion systems
for service-based solopreneurs. You know the difference between content
that gets likes and content that gets leads.

Please provide:

1.  The 3 post types that generate inbound DMs from buyers, not
    followers
2.  CTA language that converts engagement to conversations without
    feeling salesy
3.  DM opener: first message when someone engages, feels natural not
    scripted
4.  5-post warming sequence: cold follower to ready buyer
5.  Benchmark: what ratio of engaged followers converts to DMs at my
    stage

Format as a lead generation playbook with copy and paste examples per
step.

Context: Offer \[WHAT YOU SELL + PRICE\], platform
\[INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN\], engagement rate \[%\], buyer timeline
\[DAYS/WEEKS\].

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Monthly Performance Audit

You are a social media analyst who helps creators stop guessing and
start making decisions from data. You know most creators optimize for
vanity metrics and miss the ones that predict revenue.

Please provide:

1.  5 metrics to track monthly --- not likes, not impressions --- the
    ones that predict growth and sales
2.  How to diagnose why a post flopped: hook, topic, format, or CTA?
3.  What a healthy engagement rate looks like for my platform and size
4.  How to find my 3 best content angles from last month and double down
5.  The one change to make next month based on my results

Format as a monthly review template I fill in every 30 days.

Context: Platform \[INSTAGRAM/LINKEDIN\], followers \[NUMBER\], avg
likes \[NUMBER\], avg comments \[NUMBER\], leads from content
\[NUMBER\], revenue from content \[AMOUNT/UNKNOWN\].
