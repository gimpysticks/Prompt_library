# AI Prompt Structure for Content Creation

## 1. Content Ideas

**Bad Prompt:**

Give me content ideas.

**Good Prompt:**

Give me 20 content ideas for [NICHE].

**Great Prompt:**

You are my content strategist. Generate 30 content ideas for [NICHE]. Focus on my audience's biggest pain points, frustrations, fears, mistakes, objections, desires, and goals. Categorize ideas into Reach, Trust, and Sales. For each idea, include the content format, hook angle, and why it would perform well.

---

## 2. Hooks

**Bad Prompt:**

Give me 10 hooks.

**Good Prompt:**

Give me 10 negative hooks for [TOPIC].

**Great Prompt:**

Generate 30 scroll-stopping hooks for [TOPIC]. Each hook should be 5-10 words long. Focus on negative emotions, mistakes, myths, painful consequences, missed opportunities, and curiosity gaps. Avoid generic hooks and make each one feel impossible to ignore.

---

## 3. CTA

**Bad Prompt:**

Give me 5 CTA Options.

**Good Prompt:**

Give me most effective CTAs for Instagram.

**Great Prompt:**

Generate 20 CTAs for [POST TYPE]. The goal is to increase saves, shares, comments, and DMs. Keep them subtle, natural, and conversational. Avoid sounding salesy or desperate. Make readers want to engage voluntarily.

---

## 4. Captions

**Bad Prompt:**

Write a caption.

**Good Prompt:**

Write a human caption for [TOPIC].

**Great Prompt:**

Write an Instagram caption for [TOPIC]. Use a human, relatable tone. Start with a strong opening line, keep paragraphs short, use simple language, include actionable takeaways, a subtle CTA, 5 relevant hashtags, and SEO keywords at the end.

---

## 5. Scripts

**Bad Prompt:**

Write a reel script.

**Good Prompt:**

Write a reel script about [TOPIC] with a hook + CTA.

**Great Prompt:**

Act as an expert content writer. Write a 30-45 second Instagram reel script on [TOPIC]. My field is [INDUSTRY]. My position is [FOUNDER/COACH/CREATOR/etc.]. My target audience is [AUDIENCE]. The script should sound like I'm talking to a friend, use simple 5th-grade language, have a strong hook, smooth delivery, one clear takeaway, and a natural CTA. Avoid corporate or robotic language.

---

## Prompt Structure Summary

**Bad Prompt:**

Gives AI a task.

**Good Prompt:**

Gives AI a task + topic.

**Great Prompt:**

Gives AI a role, audience, objective, format, tone, and outcome.
