# 5 Instagram Growth & Monetization Prompts

## 1. Instagram Account Audit Prompt

Act as a $100,000/month Instagram social media manager. Audit my entire Instagram account based on my bio, profile, content, captions, hooks, visuals, posting strategy, and engagement. Identify everything preventing growth and give me specific fixes for each problem.

## 2. Viral Content Strategy Prompt

Analyze my Instagram niche and create a 30-day content strategy designed to maximize reach, followers, and engagement. Give me viral content ideas, powerful hooks, formats, posting frequency, and CTAs. Prioritize ideas that are easy to create and have high viral potential.

## 3. Follower Growth Prompt

Act as an elite Instagram growth strategist. Build me a step-by-step strategy to grow from my current follower count to 100K. Tell me exactly what type of Reels, carousels, Stories, hooks, CTAs, and engagement tactics I should use every day.

## 4. Sales & Monetization Prompt

Act as an Instagram monetization expert. Analyze my niche, audience, content, and existing offers, then create a strategy to turn my Instagram followers into paying customers. Recommend products, pricing, content angles, CTAs, DM strategies, and a simple sales funnel.

## 5. Complete Instagram Manager Prompt

From now on, act as my personal Instagram social media manager. Every week, analyze my performance, identify what is working and failing, suggest content improvements, create viral content ideas, optimize my profile, improve my captions and hooks, and give me a clear action plan focused on increasing views, followers, engagement, and sales.
