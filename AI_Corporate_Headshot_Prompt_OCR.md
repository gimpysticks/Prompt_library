# AI Corporate Headshot Prompt (OCR Transcription)

> **Note:** This is a transcription of the text shown in the images. It includes marketing claims from the original post that have not been independently verified.

---

## Why Use AI for a Corporate Headshot?

You need a corporate headshot. Maybe it's for LinkedIn, a new role, or a business launch.

Professional photographers may charge hundreds of dollars for a short session and a few retouched photos.

The post claims that Google Gemini can generate studio-quality corporate portraits from a single photo in about 10 seconds.

---

## Suggested Workflow

1. Open **Google Gemini**.
2. Select **Pro** mode.
3. Choose the **Create Image** tool.
4. Upload a clear photo of yourself. A selfie is said to be sufficient.

---

## Example Prompt

```text
Cinematic, ultra realistic corporate headshot of the same person from the reference image. Preserve exact facial features, skin tone, and identity.

Style: classic professional corporate portrait.

The subject is wearing a tailored dark suit, crisp white shirt, and subtle tie.

Lighting is soft, directional studio lighting with gentle shadows for depth.

Background is minimal, blurred, and neutral toned.

Shot on a high-end full-frame camera with an 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on the eyes, natural skin texture, and high dynamic range.

Expression is confident, calm, and approachable.

Ultra detailed, 8K quality, realistic color grading.
```

---

## Takeaway

The post argues that better prompts produce significantly better AI-generated portraits than generic requests like:

> "Give me a professional headshot."

According to the author, the quality difference comes more from the instructions than from the image model itself.
