# 7 Life Direction Prompt Templates

> **Note:** These prompts ask the AI to infer personality or life
> direction from a date of birth. A date of birth alone cannot reliably
> determine personality, destiny, wealth, or future events. They work
> best as reflective coaching exercises rather than factual assessments.

## 1. The Life Blueprint Decoder

**Prompt**

> I want you to act as a life-path decoder. I will give you my date of
> birth **\[Insert DOB\]**. Analyze it using psychology, numerology
> logic, and life-pattern mapping to uncover my deepest personality
> traits, hidden strengths, weaknesses, and destiny blueprint. Deliver a
> brutally honest analysis that feels like you've known me forever, and
> highlight the single biggest purpose I'm meant to pursue in this
> lifetime.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. The Soul Purpose Finder

**Prompt**

> Using my date of birth **\[Insert DOB\]**, act as my soul-purpose
> guide. Reveal the core mission of my life, the lessons I am meant to
> learn, and the contribution I am destined to make to the world. Don't
> just describe it---give me clear, actionable advice on how to align my
> daily life with this soul purpose starting today.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. The Career Destiny Finder

**Prompt**

> You are my future career mentor. Using my date of birth **\[Insert
> DOB\]**, analyze my natural talents, decision-making style, and hidden
> drives. Then reveal the three career or business paths where I am
> destined to achieve extraordinary success, along with the one field I
> should absolutely avoid at all costs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. The Relationship Destiny Map

**Prompt**

> I will give you my date of birth **\[Insert DOB\]**. Based on it,
> uncover the type of people I am most compatible with, the love lessons
> I must learn, and the role relationships play in my life journey.
> Provide an exact description of the kind of partner who will help me
> grow into my highest self.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. The Wealth & Abundance Code

**Prompt**

> Using my date of birth **\[Insert DOB\]**, decode the exact way I am
> meant to attract wealth, opportunities, and abundance in life. Reveal
> my natural money personality, the mistakes that block my financial
> growth, and the wealth-building strategy that fits my true self---not
> generic advice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. The Future Timeline Guide

**Prompt**

> I want you to use my date of birth **\[Insert DOB\]** as a time map.
> Show me the key turning points in my life journey (past, present, and
> future), the phases of growth and struggle, and the exact five-year
> path I am meant to follow. Write it like a roadmap so I can clearly
> see where I am heading.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. The Inner Genius Unlocker

**Prompt**

> You are my inner genius mentor. Based on my date of birth **\[Insert
> DOB\]**, identify the unique talent or skill that sets me apart from
> 99% of people. Then give me a practical three-step daily routine I can
> follow to unlock this genius and turn it into mastery and recognition
> in my lifetime.
