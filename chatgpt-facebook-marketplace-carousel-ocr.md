## Slide 2 — The problem

There is a pile somewhere in your house you have been meaning to deal with.

An old monitor, a bike that has not moved in two years, the box in the hall cupboard nobody opens.

Selling it means photographing everything, working out what each item is worth, writing the listings, then answering messages for weeks.

That is why it is still there.

---

## Slide 3 — Photograph and upload

Photograph everything you want to sell.

Bad lighting is fine, these are not product shots.

Then open ChatGPT, select Agent mode from the model picker at the top, and upload the photos.

Agent mode is the version that can operate a real browser on its own instead of just writing text back to you.

---

## Slide 4 — 1. Price Everything

> Look at each one of these items. Tell me exactly what they are, what they're worth right now, and how much I could realistically sell them for.

It reads the photos, identifies each item, and values it against what similar things are actually selling for today.

---

## Slide 5 — 2. Write The Listings

> Now write me the perfect listing for each one. A title that sells, a full description, and the exact price to post it at.

Stay in the same chat.

It still has the photos and the prices in front of it, so every listing comes back ready to post.

---

## Slide 6 — 3. Let It Post Them

> Open up your own browser and go create these listings on Facebook Marketplace for me, using the photos and descriptions you just wrote.

This is the part most people do not know it can do.

A browser window opens on screen and it works through the listings one by one while you watch.

---

## Slide 7 — 4. Let It Handle The Buyers

> Now schedule a task that runs every 24 hours to respond to the buyers and negotiate the best possible price for me.

Scheduled tasks are what make this stick.

It checks the messages on its own instead of waiting for you to remember.

---

## Slide 8 — Limitations

It is not fully hands off.

Facebook will ask for a login code and will sometimes show a security check, and you have to type those in yourself while the agent waits.

It also gets prices wrong on anything unusual.

It is reliable on common items with a clear resale market and it guesses on the rest.

Read the listings before it posts them.

---

## Slide 9 — Why the effort may be worthwhile

The stuff in that pile has a real number on it.

A few hundred dollars for most people, more if there is a bike or a camera lens in there.

It sat there because the work of getting that money was worth more than the money.

Four prompts and one afternoon is a very different trade than four weekends of photographing, listing and haggling.
