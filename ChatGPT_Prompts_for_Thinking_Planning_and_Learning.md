# ChatGPT Prompts for Thinking, Planning, and Learning

The supplied carousel includes the cover and prompts 1–9. The cover advertises 10 prompts. Prompt 10 was absent from the supplied images.

>
> USE THESE 10 PROMPTS INSTEAD
>
> SWIPE FOR MORE

## 1. The Thinking Partner

> Don't answer my question right away. First, identify what I'm really trying to solve. Ask me the 3 most important questions that would help you give me a much better answer.

## 2. The Ruthless Critic

> I'm going to run an idea by you. Your job isn't to agree with me. Find its 5 biggest weaknesses, challenge my assumptions, spot what I'm missing, and tell me what could make it fail.

## 3. The Reverse Engineer

> I want to achieve [SPECIFIC RESULT]. Start with the desired outcome and work backward. Identify precisely the steps, skills, tools, decisions, and milestones needed to get there. Explain the most efficient path for me.

## 4. The Expert Simulator

> Act as a top 1% expert in [FIELD]. Evaluate my current approach, identify what beginners get wrong, explain what experienced pros do differently, and show me how to think at an advanced level.

## 5. The Decision Architect

> I'm torn between [OPTION A] and [OPTION B]. Build a decision matrix that compares the two options based on my priorities, risks, opportunity costs, and long-term consequences. Identify the option most likely to produce the best outcome.

## 6. The Opportunity Hunter

> Analyze [INDUSTRY, SKILL, OR BUSINESS]. Identify 10 overlooked opportunities that most people don't pay attention to. Explain why each one exists, who would be willing to pay for it, and how I could practically take advantage of it.

## 7. The Learning Accelerator

> I want to master [SKILL] as efficiently as possible. Build me a learning system based on deliberate practice, active recall, spaced repetition, and hands-on projects. Focus on the knowledge that offers the greatest practical advantage.

## 8. The System Builder

> Help me consistently achieve [GOAL]. Instead of giving me motivation or random advice, design a repeatable system with daily actions, triggers, checkpoints, feedback loops, and measurable progress.

## 9. The Knowledge Extractor

> I'm going to give you an [ARTICLE, BOOK, TRANSCRIPT, OR DOCUMENT]. Don't just summarize it. Extract the most valuable insights, implicit assumptions, practical applications, counterintuitive lessons, and ideas I can apply right away.
