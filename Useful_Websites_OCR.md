# Useful Websites

## 1. FlightRadar24

Shows live locations of every airplane in the sky right now.

-   Track specific flights in real time.
-   View altitude, speed, and route.
-   Completely free.

Website: https://flightradar24.com

## 2. 10 Minute Mail

Provides a temporary email address that expires automatically.

-   Use it when a website asks for your email but you don't want to give
    it.
-   No signup needed.

Website: https://10minutemail.com

## 3. Wayback Machine

Search any website and see how it looked in the past.

-   Useful for finding content that was deleted or changed.
-   Over 860 billion pages archived.

Website: https://web.archive.org

## 4. Temp-Number

Provides a temporary foreign phone number for free.

-   Receive SMS verification codes without giving out your real number.

Website: https://temp-number.com

## 5. Have I Been Pwned

Check whether your email address or password has appeared in a data
breach.

-   Enter your email.
-   See which breaches exposed your information and what data was
    leaked.

Website: https://haveibeenpwned.com

## 6. BugMeNot

Provides shared login details for websites that require an account.

-   Access content without registering.
-   Commonly works on news sites and forums.

Website: https://bugmenot.com

## 7. Whois

Look up who owns any website or domain.

-   Shows creation date.
-   Shows registrant information (when available).
-   Shows expiration date and hosting details.

Website: https://whois.domaintools.com

## 8. Down For Everyone Or Just Me

Checks whether a website is down globally or just for you.

-   Helps determine whether the issue is your connection or the website.

Website: https://downforeveryoneorjustme.com

## 9. Just Delete Me

Shows how difficult it is to delete your account on major websites.

-   Rates services as Easy, Medium, Hard, or Impossible.
-   Links directly to the account deletion page.

Website: https://justdeleteme.xyz

## 10. FutureMe

Write an email to your future self and choose when it will be delivered.

-   Useful for goals, reminders, or messages to yourself years in the
    future.

Website: https://futureme.org

## 11. Remove.bg

Automatically removes the background from an image.

-   Upload a photo.
-   Background removed in seconds.
-   No Photoshop or editing skills required.
-   Free.

Website: https://remove.bg

## 12. WindowSwap

Watch the live view from a random window somewhere in the world.

-   People upload views from their windows.
-   Sit back and watch the world go by.

Website: https://window-swap.com

## 13. Privnote

Send a note that deletes itself after it has been read.

-   One read and it's gone forever.
-   No account required.

Website: https://privnote.com
