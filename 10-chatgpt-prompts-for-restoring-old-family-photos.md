# 10 ChatGPT Prompts for Restoring Old Family Photos

Source: @create_daniel2

## Introduction

**Breaking News:**

ChatGPT can now restore your old family photos like a professional restorer.

Scan or snap an old photo and use these 10 prompts:

## 1. Bring Back a Faded Photo

> Restore this faded photo. Bring back the original colours and contrast, keep every face and detail exactly as it is, and don't make it look like a modern photo.

## 2. Repair Cracks and Creases

> Remove the cracks, creases, scratches and dust from this photo. Rebuild only the damaged areas so they match what's around them. Change nothing else.

## 3. Colourise a Black and White Photo

> Colourise this black and white photo with realistic colours for the era it was taken in. Natural skin tones, muted clothing colours, nothing too bright.

## 4. Sharpen a Blurry Face

> Sharpen the faces in this photo so they're clear, but keep them looking like the same people. Don't change their features, age or expression.

## 5. Fix a Torn Corner

> Part of this photo is torn or missing. Rebuild the missing area so it blends in with the rest. Keep it simple and don't invent new people or objects.

## 6. Remove a Stain or Tape Mark

> Remove the stain and tape marks from this photo. Restore the area underneath so it looks untouched. Leave the rest of the photo exactly as it is.

## 7. Get It Ready to Print

> Prepare this restored photo for printing at [A4 or 8x10]. Increase the sharpness and resolution, keep the grain natural, and don't smooth out skin texture.

## 8. Make It a Wedding-Day Portrait Again

> Restore this old wedding photo so it looks like the day it was printed. Bright whites, clean background, original framing. Keep both faces exactly as they are.

## 9. Fix the Colour Cast

> This photo has turned orange, green or purple with age. Correct the colours so whites look white and skin looks natural. Don't change anything else.

## 10. Turn It Into a Gift

> Turn this restored photo into a framed print design with a simple white border and a small caption at the bottom that says [NAMES AND YEAR].
