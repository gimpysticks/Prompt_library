## Introduction

**What happens when you ask ChatGPT to build your entire family tree and
find relatives you never knew existed?**

I tried it. It came back with family members I had no idea I was related
to.

**Here are the 4 prompts:**

## 1. Build The Starting Tree

It interviews you first, then maps everything you already know.

**Paste to ChatGPT:**

> I want to build my full family tree. Ask me everything you need to
> know. My full name, where my family is from, and every relative I can
> remember.

Answer its questions and it lays out a clean tree from what you've got.

## 2. Trace Your Family Line

Stay in the same chat. This pulls the history sitting behind your
surname.

**Paste to ChatGPT:**

> Now, based on my last name and where my family came from, tell me the
> origins of my family line and how far back you can trace it.

## 3. Find The Relatives You Don't Know About

Turn on web search or agent mode first, or it can't go anywhere.

**Paste to ChatGPT:**

> Using everything you know about me, search public records, obituaries
> and free genealogy sites for relatives connected to my family that I
> don't already have.

It opens its own browser and works through whatever it can reach. Names
come back that you've never heard in your life.

## 4. Build The Visual Tree

Turns all of it into a proper chart instead of a list.

**Paste to ChatGPT:**

> Now build me the full visual family tree with everyone you found and
> how we're all connected.

Ask for it as a downloadable file and you get something you can send
round the family.
